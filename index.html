AE-Timer

Problem: a study day needs more than one countdown — and a timer that deletes itself when it finishes is no use the next day.

AE-Timer is a multi-board timer app: boards with independent, concurrent timers, board totals, daily / weekly / monthly history, and JSON export and import, so progress is never locked inside one session. Timers finish and reset instead of disappearing.

AyaatSync

Problem: finding a verse that speaks to how you feel right now, without searching through a whole text.

AyaatSync is a mood-based Qur'an verse app. It holds 80 ayats — 20 for each of four moods (Happy, Sad, Angry, Anxious) — each shown with its original Arabic script, an English translation, and its Surah reference.

DjMC Exam Todo

DjMC Exam Todo

Problem: a 29-day medical exam plan with 60 college items is impossible to hold in your head — or in a notebook that never reminds you what comes next.

An exam-oriented task page for Dinajpur Medical College's second professional exams: 140 day tasks across 29 days, plus a 60-item tracker with five revision stages per item, a countdown to both exams, and progress saved on your device with JSON export and import.
